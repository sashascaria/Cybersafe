Phishing

Scammers try to trick you into giving away your information. Learn how to spot them!

What is phishing?

Phishing is when someone pretends to be a trusted person or company to trick you into giving them your personal information — like your password, address, or credit card number.

The name comes from "fishing" — scammers cast out fake messages and wait for someone to take the bait!

Warning signs to watch for 🚨

  • 📧 Emails or messages asking for your password or personal details
  • ⚡ Messages that say "Act NOW or your account will be deleted!"
  • 🔗 Links that look odd or slightly misspelled — like "g00gle.com"
  • 🎁 Messages saying you have won a prize you never entered
  • 👤 Messages from unknown senders pretending to be your bank or school

What to do if you think something is fake

  • 🛑 Do not click any links in the message
  • 🗑️ Delete the message immediately
  • 👨‍👩‍👧 Tell a trusted adult straight away
  • 🔍 If unsure, go to the real website by typing it yourself — never click the link
