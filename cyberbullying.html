Cyberbullying

Everyone deserves to feel safe online. Learn what cyberbullying is and how to stop it.

What is cyberbullying?

Cyberbullying is when someone uses technology — like phones, apps, or social media — to hurt, embarrass, threaten, or harass another person on purpose.

It can happen to anyone and it is never okay. If you are being cyberbullied, it is not your fault.

Examples of cyberbullying 📱

  • 💬 Sending mean or hurtful messages
  • 📸 Sharing embarrassing photos or videos without permission
  • 👥 Leaving someone out of group chats on purpose to upset them
  • 🎭 Pretending to be someone else online to spread lies
  • 😠 Posting unkind comments on someone's posts repeatedly

What to do if it happens to you

  • 🛑 Do not reply to the bully — it often makes things worse
  • 📸 Take a screenshot as evidence before deleting messages
  • 🚫 Block the person on the platform
  • 👨‍👩‍👧 Tell a trusted adult — a parent, teacher, or school counsellor
  • 💙 Remember: it is not your fault and you are not alone
