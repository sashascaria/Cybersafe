Online Privacy

Your personal information is precious. Learn what to keep private online!

What is online privacy?

Online privacy means keeping your personal information safe when you use the internet. Not everyone online is who they say they are — some people may try to use your information to harm you.

The good news is that by knowing what to share and what to keep private, you can stay much safer online!

Information you should NEVER share online 🚫

  • 🏠 Your home address or school name
  • 📞 Your phone number
  • 🎂 Your full date of birth
  • 📸 Photos that show where you live or go to school
  • 🔑 Any passwords or account details
  • 💳 Any family financial information

How to protect your privacy online

  • 🔒 Set your social media accounts to private
  • 👥 Only accept friend requests from people you know in real life
  • 🤔 Think before you post — once something is online, it can stay there forever
  • 👨‍👩‍👧 Talk to a trusted adult if anyone online makes you uncomfortable
