About This Site

Why I built CyberSafe Teens and the story behind it.

Why I made this

My name is Sasha Scaria and I am a student at GEMS Modern Academy in Dubai. I built this website as my MYP Personal Project. I chose cybersecurity because something happened to me that really made me realise how important it is.

A few years ago, one of my online accounts was hacked. Someone got into it and I had no idea how. It was a really scary and stressful experience. I had to change everything and I felt like I had no control. After that, I started learning more about how these attacks actually happen and what I could have done to prevent it.

What surprised me most was how simple the solutions are. Things like using a strong password, turning on two-factor authentication, and not clicking unknown links can stop most attacks. But a lot of people, especially kids and teens, just do not know about them yet.

Who this site is for

I designed CyberSafe Teens for young people aged 8 and above. I wanted the content to be simple and easy to understand without being boring or too basic. Each topic has a short explanation and a quiz so you can test what you have learned.

I also built the site myself using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Bootstrap. Learning to code was one of the biggest challenges of this project and it took a lot of trial and error to get things like the card layout and the quiz working properly.

What I hope you take away

You do not have to be a tech expert to stay safe online. A few simple habits can make a huge difference. I hope that after going through this site, you feel more confident about protecting yourself and know what to do if something goes wrong.

If you have any feedback on the site, I would love to hear it. This project means a lot to me and I hope it actually helps someone.
