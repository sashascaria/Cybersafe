About This Project

Why I built CyberSafe Teens and what I hope it does for you.

Why I made this website

My name is Sasha and I am a student at GEMS Modern Academy in Dubai. I built CyberSafe Teens as my IB MYP Personal Project.

Growing up in a household where cybersecurity is discussed every day, I became aware early on of how many young people are vulnerable online — not because they are careless, but simply because nobody taught them what to look out for.

I also experienced this personally. After downloading a file without checking its source, I was hacked across nearly all of my accounts including Instagram and Gmail. It was a stressful and upsetting experience — and it made me realise that if it could happen to me, it could happen to anyone.

I built this website to give young people the knowledge they need to stay safe online — in a way that is simple, engaging, and actually makes sense for their age.

What I learned building this 💡

  • 💻 How to build a website from scratch using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
  • 🎨 How to design for a young audience — keeping things visual, simple, and friendly
  • 🔐 How to research and simplify complex cybersecurity topics
  • 🧩 How to use Bootstrap to make a website work on different screen sizes
  • 🧠 How to write JavaScript to power interactive quizzes
