Password Safety

A strong password is your first line of defence online. Learn how to make one!

What is a password?

A password is a secret code that lets you into your accounts — like email, games, and social media. If someone else gets your password, they can get into your account and pretend to be you.

That is why it is really important to make sure your password is strong and hard to guess!

What makes a strong password? ✅

  • 🔡 Use at least 8 characters — the longer the better
  • 🔢 Mix uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols
  • 🚫 Never use your name, birthday, or pet's name
  • 🔁 Use a different password for every account
  • 🤫 Never share your password — not even with your best friend
  • 💡 Try using a passphrase — 3 random words joined together, like BlueFrogJumps!
