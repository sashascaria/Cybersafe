Online Scams

Scammers are clever — but you can be cleverer! Learn how to spot their tricks.

What is an online scam?

An online scam is when someone tries to trick you into giving them money or personal information by pretending to offer something great — like a free gift, a job, or a special deal.

Remember: if something sounds too good to be true, it probably is!

Common types of scams 🎭

  • 🎁 Prize scams — "You've won! Just pay a small fee to claim your prize"
  • 💰 Money scams — Someone claims they need your help to move money and will pay you
  • 🎮 Gaming scams — Fake websites offering free V-Bucks, Robux, or game items
  • ❤️ Friendship scams — Someone pretends to be your friend then asks for money or gifts
  • 🛍️ Shopping scams — Fake online shops that take your money and send nothing

How to stay scam-safe

  • 🤔 Always ask — "Why would a stranger give me something for free?"
  • 👨‍👩‍👧 Never share payment information — always ask a trusted adult first
  • 🔍 Check if a website is real before buying — look for reviews and a padlock icon
  • 🚨 Report scams to a trusted adult immediately
